01 Our story

A Surat textile house, forty years in the making.

Ruchi Group started in Surat in 1985 making shirting fabric. That was the whole business, and we set out to do it properly. Today we also make dress materials, sarees and home furnishing textiles, on modern looms, with our own team of designers, artists and production staff.

“ Our resolution to keep striving hard to deliver quality finished goods with versatile designs is the secret to our achievement.

That habit is what grew a single-product mill into an integrated fabric house. Today vendors in India and abroad rely on us for consistent quality, fresh design and dependable capacity.

02 The journey

1985

Growth

Capability

Today

03 What we make

Five product lines.
One standard.

01 / 05

01

Plain Woven Fabrics

Our foundation fabric. Clean and versatile, made in large volumes for shirting, kurtas and everyday garments, with the same quality from the first roll to the last.

  • Shirting
  • Kurtas
  • Garments
02

Textured / Dobby

Self-designed weaves that change how a fabric feels, falls and catches the light, with no printing at all. Small, understated patterns for menswear and womenswear.

  • Self-design
  • Menswear
  • Womenswear
03

Jacquard Brocades

Ornamental weaving for borders, laces and garment detailing, in all-over or panel layouts. A single design can carry up to eight colours.

  • Borders
  • Panels
  • 8 colours
04

Jacquard Sarees

Art-silk jacquards on silk, raw silk, organza and viscose bases, drawing on Banarasi, Kanjivaram and Paithani traditions.

  • Silk
  • Organza
  • Viscose
05

Jacquard Dupattas

Fancy jacquard dupattas in dyeable and top-dyed versions, designed to finish an outfit, not just go with it.

  • Dyeable
  • Top-dyed

05 How it's made

Everything under
one roof.

Design, weaving, finishing and dispatch all happen in-house. That's how we keep quality steady from the first metre to the eighteen millionth.

Step 01 · Design
01

Design & Merchandising

Every new range starts here. Our designers and artists work out the weaves, motifs and colourways buyers will be asking for next season.

02

Weaving

Patterns are set on modern jacquard and dobby looms, thread by thread, with up to eight colours in a single fabric.

03

Finishing & Quality

Every roll is checked for weave, weight and colour before it carries the Ruchi name.

04

Warehouse & Dispatch

Finished fabric is sorted and stored in our own warehouse at Pandesara GIDC, ready to ship at short notice.
